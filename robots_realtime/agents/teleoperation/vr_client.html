Instructions

  1. Open this page on your Quest browser (HTTP, developer mode required).
  2. Connect automatically — the page reconnects every 2 s if the socket drops.
  3. Press Enter VR, then hold your controllers in a comfortable resting pose and press Calibrate (or hold both grip buttons for 0.5 s inside VR).
  4. Move your hands — the robot arms track your controllers in relative mode.
  5. Squeeze the grip button to close the gripper.
  6. Re-calibrate any time you want to reset the reference frame.
